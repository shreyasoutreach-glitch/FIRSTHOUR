# PRIMHORA Product Walkthrough

This walkthrough is for a finance/AP operator evaluating PRIMHORA.

## 1. Boundary
Open the landing page and say: "PRIMHORA is read-only. It does not move or recover money. It reconstructs what happened from financial records and evidence, shows what is verified, and packages the case for an authorized human."

Choose Explore the demo. All demo financial data must remain visibly labelled synthetic.

## 2. Synthetic case
Use fictional company and account details. Do not enter real banking credentials. The demo account is not connected to a bank or payment provider.

## 3. Evidence
Show artifact, source hash, extraction method, candidate claims and verification state. Explain that an extracted amount is a claim, not financial truth, until verified against source financial events.

## 4. Reconstruction
Show source financial events, communications, beneficiary changes, evidence references and unresolved questions. The timeline is assembled from deterministic records, not invented as an LLM narrative.

## 5. Graph and exposure
Show source-backed relationships between merchant, payout, beneficiary, fund account, communication and evidence.

## 6. Human context
Ask: "Did your team authorize these payouts?" Record the answer. Show that human context is stored separately and does not rewrite financial records.

## 7. Evidence packet
Show case identifier, source-backed chronology, transaction references, evidence index, verification states, outstanding questions, deterministic exposure, audit history and the explicit read-only boundary.

## 8. Customer workflow
customer export -> validation -> normalization -> deterministic reconciliation -> incident -> evidence -> human context -> packet.

The customer workflow must never inherit seeded demo incidents.

## 9. Buyer discovery
Ask how long the investigation takes, which systems are involved, which facts are hardest to prove, which documents are missing, who approves the case and what must be sent to a bank, auditor, insurer or internal investigation team.

Do not claim savings until measured with the customer's workflow.
