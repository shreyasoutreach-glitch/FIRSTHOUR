# FIRST HOUR: CUSTOMER ZERO OFFER

## THE CUSTOMER ZERO PROPOSITION

FIRST HOUR reconstructs financial attacks from fragmented event data, identifies where legitimate behavior first diverged, maps connected exposure, and gives investigators a recovery-ready view before an incident becomes a larger loss.

We want to prove this on your data, without touching your production infrastructure.

### YOU PROVIDE:
- 30 to 90 days of historical event telemetry (CSV, JSON, or JSONL).
- Account identifiers, transaction amounts, timestamps, and destination relationships.
- (Optional but recommended) Historical fraud labels for comparison.
- Note: Identifiers may be securely hashed (e.g., HMAC) prior to handoff.

### FIRST HOUR RETURNS:
1. **Reconstructed Incidents:** A chronological map of compromised accounts.
2. **First Divergence:** The exact timestamp legitimate behavior mathematically broke baseline.
3. **Incremental Detection Lead Time:** How much earlier FIRST HOUR flagged the divergence compared to your existing historical controls.
4. **Connected Exposure:** A graph analysis of fan-in/fan-out mule networks.
5. **Potential Exposure Prevented:** Simulated financial loss avoidance.
6. **Executive Report:** A polished presentation of findings.

### MODE OF OPERATION:
- **Historical Replay Only:** We replay your data strictly point-in-time.
- **Shadow Mode:** FIRST HOUR operates in a read-only environment.
- **Zero Production Risk:** No real money movement, no account blocking, no credential changes, and no live API integration required.

You understand exactly what FIRST HOUR detects before writing a line of code.
