# FIRST HOUR: OBJECTION HANDLER

**"Why can't our existing fraud system do this?"**
FIRST HOUR is designed to complement existing fraud controls by reconstructing incidents across time, entities and transactions. Customer Zero measures whether FIRST HOUR adds incremental detection or investigation value alongside existing controls.

**"Is this just another anomaly detector?"**
No. Pure anomaly detection creates unmanageable false positive queues. FIRST HOUR is an Attack Reconstruction platform. We don't just alert; we calculate First Divergence, map the connected blast radius, and synthesize an incident timeline for investigators.

**"Do you need production access?"**
No. Customer Zero is a historical, read-only shadow evaluation. We do not touch your live APIs or your production ledger.

**"Do you need PII?"**
No. We require unique identifiers to build graph structures and baselines, but you can securely hash (e.g., HMAC) all account IDs, IPs, and device IDs before handoff.

**"Will this block customers?"**
No. During Customer Zero, FIRST HOUR cannot block anyone. It only simulates interventions to measure potential ROI.

**"How accurate is it?"**
FIRST HOUR has been synthetically validated to reduce massive exposure, but real-world accuracy and calibration on your specific fraud taxonomy is exactly what the Customer Zero evaluation is designed to establish. We don't guess your accuracy; we measure it on your historical data.

**"Can you guarantee savings?"**
No. We calculate potential simulated exposure prevented. Real savings depend on how quickly your operational teams execute the interventions FIRST HOUR recommends.

**"Why should we trust an AI system?"**
FIRST HOUR uses deterministic ledger math for all risk scores, graph traversals, and intervention policies. Generative AI is strictly isolated to summarizing messy telemetry into human-readable timelines. The AI cannot authorize a block or move money.

**"What happens if your model is wrong?"**
If FIRST HOUR generates a False Positive in shadow mode, it gets flagged in the Customer Zero friction report. Our policy is designed to accumulate evidence before recommending intervention, minimizing unnecessary friction on normal variance.

**"How long does integration take?"**
Customer Zero does not require production integration. A historical CSV/JSON/JSONL export is sufficient to begin evaluation. 

**"Can you run against historical data only?"**
Yes, that is the explicit design of the Customer Zero pilot.

**"Why don't you just replace our fraud system?"**
FIRST HOUR operates best as an intelligent orchestration layer on top of your existing controls. It catches the sophisticated, multi-stage attacks that slip past traditional rules engines.
