# FIRST HOUR: DISCOVERY QUESTIONNAIRE

*(Note: Customers are not required to answer every question to qualify for a pilot. This is an investigative guide to frame the engagement.)*

1. What fraud types create the largest losses today?
2. What percentage of fraud is discovered after transaction completion?
3. What telemetry is currently available in your data warehouse?
4. Do you retain device, session, and IP information?
5. Do you retain beneficiary/VPA relationships?
6. How long is historical event data retained?
7. Do you have confirmed fraud labels?
8. Do you record the timestamp when existing controls intervene?
9. How do investigators currently reconstruct incidents?
10. How long does a typical manual investigation take?
11. What is the approximate cost of a manual review?
12. What systems currently provide fraud alerts?
13. Where do investigators believe current controls struggle the most?
14. Can historical data be exported securely in CSV, JSON, or JSONL?
