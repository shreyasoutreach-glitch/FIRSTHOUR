# FIRST HOUR: EXECUTIVE PITCH

## HEADLINE
Financial attacks leave a trail before they become a loss.

## PROBLEM
FIRST HOUR is designed to complement existing fraud controls by reconstructing incidents across time, entities and transactions. Investigators need to reconstruct the attack timeline and map connected beneficiaries. By the time the full blast radius is mapped manually, the capital may be gone.

## WHAT FIRST HOUR DOES

### FIRST DIVERGENCE
Where did legitimate behavior first break? FIRST HOUR locates the identified divergence point from an account's historical baseline.

### INCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION
What actually happened, in chronological order? We fuse identity, behavior, network, and sequence telemetry into a unified timeline.

### GRAPH EXPOSURE
Which accounts, beneficiaries, and transactions are connected? We map fan-in (mules) and fan-out structures dynamically.

### FINANCIAL EXPOSURE
How much capital was exposed before intervention? We calculate potential loss and simulate recovery options.

### INCREMENTAL VALUE
Did FIRST HOUR see it earlier or see something existing controls missed? We compare our detection lead time directly against your legacy alerts.

## RUN A CUSTOMER ZERO
Give us 30-90 days of historical fraud telemetry. We replay it in Shadow Mode and measure whether FIRST HOUR finds incidents earlier, finds incidents existing controls missed, or reveals a larger connected blast radius.
